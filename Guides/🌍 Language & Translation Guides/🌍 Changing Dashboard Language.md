# 🌍 Changing Dashboard Language

The World Cup 2026 Dashboard includes built-in multi-language support, allowing users to switch between supported languages directly from Home Assistant.

---

## Supported Languages

The dashboard currently supports:

* English 🇬🇧
* French 🇫🇷
* German 🇩🇪
* Spanish 🇪🇸
* Italian 🇮🇹
* Dutch 🇳🇱
* Portuguese 🇵🇹
* Arabic 🇸🇦
* Japanese 🇯🇵
* Korean 🇰🇷
* Swedish 🇸🇪
* Norwegian 🇳🇴

---

## Changing The Language

Open your World Cup Dashboard and locate the language selector.

Select your preferred language from the dropdown menu.

The dashboard will automatically update supported labels, navigation items and page content.

No restart is required.

---

## World Cup Application

Language selection also applies to the dedicated World Cup Application, including:

* Overview
* Live Centre
* Fixtures & Results
* Group Standings
* Knockout Centre
* Statistics Hub
* Tournament Records
* Stadium & Venues

---

## Troubleshooting

### Language Not Updating

Refresh your browser:

```text
Ctrl + F5
```

### Language Selector Missing

Verify the language helper exists:

```text
input_select.world_cup_dashboard_language
```
