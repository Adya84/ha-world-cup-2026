# 🏟️ Stadium & Venues Guide

The Stadium & Venues page provides information about the host stadiums and cities being used during FIFA World Cup 2026.

With matches being played across the United States, Canada and Mexico, this page allows you to explore tournament venues and host locations directly from Home Assistant.

---

# Accessing The Stadium & Venues Page

Open the World Cup 2026 Application from the Home Assistant sidebar.

Select:

```text
Stadiums & Venues
```

from the navigation menu.

---

# What The Stadium & Venues Page Shows

The page provides venue information for World Cup 2026.

Information may include:

* Stadium Name
* Host City
* Host Country
* Stadium Capacity
* Match Venue Information
* Final Venue Information

---

# Troubleshooting

## No Stadiums Showing

Verify the World Cup 2026 integration is loaded correctly.

Navigate to:

```text
Settings → Devices & Services
```

and confirm the integration is active.

---

## Missing Venue Information

Some fixtures may not yet have confirmed venue information available.

Venue data will appear automatically when provided.

---

## Final Venue Not Showing

Ensure tournament data has successfully loaded and refresh your browser:

```text
Ctrl + F5
```

---

## Page Appears Empty

Reload the integration and refresh the application.
