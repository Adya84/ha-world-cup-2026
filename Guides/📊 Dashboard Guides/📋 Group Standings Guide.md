# 📋 Group Standings Guide

The Group Standings page provides a complete overview of all FIFA World Cup 2026 groups.

Track team positions, points, goal difference and qualification status throughout the tournament.

With 48 teams and 12 groups, the Group Standings page is one of the most important areas of the World Cup 2026 Application.

---

# Accessing Group Standings

Open the World Cup 2026 Application from the Home Assistant sidebar.

Select:

```text
Group Standings
```

from the navigation menu.

---

# What The Group Standings Page Shows

The page displays all tournament groups and the teams within each group.

Information may include:

* Team Name
* Matches Played
* Wins
* Draws
* Losses
* Goals For
* Goals Against
* Goal Difference
* Points
* Current Position

---

# Group Format

World Cup 2026 features:

```text
12 Groups
```

with:

```text
4 Teams Per Group
```

The top teams progress to the knockout stages.

---

# Team Position

Teams are ranked automatically based on official FIFA tournament rules.

Example:

```text
1. England
2. USA
3. Japan
4. Ghana
```

Positions update automatically after each completed match.

---

# Matches Played

Displays how many group matches each team has played.

Example:

```text
Played: 2
```

---

# Wins, Draws & Losses

Track team performance throughout the group stage.

Example:

```text
Wins: 2
Draws: 0
Losses: 0
```

---

# Goals For & Goals Against

Displays:

```text
Goals For (GF)
```

and

```text
Goals Against (GA)
```

for each team.

Example:

```text
GF: 5
GA: 1
```

---

# Goal Difference

Calculated automatically:

```text
Goals For - Goals Against
```

Example:

```text
+4
```

Goal difference is often used to separate teams on equal points.

---

# Points

Teams earn:

```text
Win = 3 Points
Draw = 1 Point
Loss = 0 Points
```

Example:

```text
Points: 6
```

---

# Troubleshooting

## Standings Not Showing

Verify the integration is loaded correctly.

Navigate to:

```text
Settings → Devices & Services
```

and confirm World Cup 2026 is active.

---

## Groups Appear Empty

This may occur when:

* The tournament has not started
* Data has not yet loaded
* Demo Mode is disabled

---

## Standings Not Updating

Refresh your browser:

```text
Ctrl + F5
```

or reload the integration.

---

## Positions Appear Incorrect

Standings are calculated using official tournament data and FIFA ranking rules.

Updates may take a few minutes following match completion.
