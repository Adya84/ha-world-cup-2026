# 🏅 Records Page Guide

The Records Page tracks the most notable achievements and milestones throughout FIFA World Cup 2026.

As the tournament progresses, records update automatically to highlight standout performances, high-scoring matches and team achievements.

---

# Accessing The Records Page

Open the World Cup 2026 Application from the Home Assistant sidebar.

Select:

```text
Records
```

from the navigation menu.

---

# What The Records Page Shows

The Records Page provides a collection of tournament achievements and record-breaking performances.

Information may include:

* Biggest Win
* Highest Scoring Match
* Top Scoring Team
* Best Defence
* Latest Result
* Clean Sheet Leaders
* Team Goal Statistics

---

# Biggest Win

Tracks the largest winning margin recorded during the tournament.

Example:

```text
Spain 6 - 0 New Zealand
```

The record automatically updates if a larger winning margin occurs.

---

# Highest Scoring Match

Tracks the match with the most goals scored.

Example:

```text
Brazil 5 - 4 Germany
```

Useful for identifying the most entertaining matches of the tournament.

---

# Top Scoring Team

Displays the team that has scored the most goals during the tournament.

Example:

```text
Argentina
18 Goals
```

Updates automatically after each completed fixture.

---

# Best Defence

Displays the team that has conceded the fewest goals.

Example:

```text
France
2 Goals Conceded
```

A useful indicator of defensive performance.

---

# Latest Result

Shows the most recently completed match.

Example:

```text
England 2 - 1 Portugal
```

This updates automatically as fixtures finish.

---

# Clean Sheet Leaders

Tracks teams that have recorded the most clean sheets.

Example:

```text
Spain
4 Clean Sheets
```

---

# Team Goal Statistics

Displays additional attacking and defensive information.

May include:

* Goals Scored
* Goals Conceded
* Goal Difference
* Average Goals Per Match

---

# Record Updates

Records are recalculated automatically whenever:

* A match finishes
* Scores are updated
* New tournament data becomes available

No manual updates are required.

# Troubleshooting

## Records Showing Empty Values

This may occur when:

* No matches have been completed
* Tournament data has not loaded
* The tournament has not started

---

## Records Not Updating

Refresh your browser:

```text
Ctrl + F5
```

or reload the integration.

---

## Statistics Appear Incorrect

Records are calculated using official tournament data and will update automatically after future refreshes.
