# 🏆 Knockout Centre Guide

The Knockout Centre provides complete coverage of the FIFA World Cup 2026 knockout stages.

Track qualified teams, knockout fixtures, results and the journey to the World Cup Final.

Once the group stage concludes, the Knockout Centre becomes the primary location for following the tournament.

---

# Accessing The Knockout Centre

Open the World Cup 2026 Application from the Home Assistant sidebar.

Select:

```text
Knockout Centre
```

from the navigation menu.

---

# What The Knockout Centre Shows

The Knockout Centre displays all elimination-round fixtures and results.

Information may include:

* Qualified Teams
* Knockout Fixtures
* Match Results
* Tournament Progression
* Extra Time Matches
* Penalty Shootouts
* Finalists
* World Cup Final Information

---

# Tournament Structure

The FIFA World Cup 2026 knockout stages include:

```text
Round of 32
```

↓

```text
Round of 16
```

↓

```text
Quarter Finals
```

↓

```text
Semi Finals
```

↓

```text
Third Place Playoff
```

↓

```text
Final
```

---

# Round Of 32

The Round of 32 is the first knockout round.

Qualified teams from the group stage progress automatically.

Each fixture is win-or-go-home.

Example:

```text
England vs Mexico
```

---

# Round Of 16

The remaining teams progress to the Round of 16.

Example:

```text
Brazil vs Germany
```

Only the winning team advances.

---

# Quarter Finals

The final eight teams compete for a place in the Semi Finals.

Example:

```text
Argentina vs France
```

---

# Semi Finals

The final four teams battle for a place in the World Cup Final.

Example:

```text
Spain vs England
```

---

# Third Place Playoff

The losing Semi Finalists compete for third place.

Example:

```text
Germany vs Portugal
```

---

# World Cup Final

The final match of the tournament.

Example:

```text
England vs Brazil
```

The winner becomes:

🏆 FIFA World Cup 2026 Champion

---

# Match Results

Completed knockout fixtures display:

* Final Score
* Extra Time Results
* Penalty Shootout Results

Example:

```text
England 2 - 2 Brazil
England win 5-4 on penalties
```

---

# Extra Time Tracking

The Knockout Centre tracks matches requiring extra time.

Example:

```text
AET
```

Meaning:

```text
After Extra Time
```

---

# Penalty Shootout Tracking

Matches decided by penalties are automatically identified.

Example:

```text
PEN
```

Meaning:

```text
Penalty Shootout
```

---

# Qualified Teams

As the tournament progresses, the Knockout Centre automatically updates qualified teams.

This allows users to follow the route to the final.

---

# Eliminated Teams

Teams are removed from progression tracking once eliminated.

This provides a clear picture of the remaining contenders.

---

# Tournament Progression

The Knockout Centre makes it easy to see:

Knockout data updates automatically as:

* Fixtures are confirmed
* Matches are completed

# Troubleshooting

## No Knockout Fixtures Showing

This is normal before the knockout stages begin.

Knockout fixtures appear once qualification has been confirmed.

---

## Missing Teams

Teams only appear once qualification has been officially determined.

---

## Results Not Updating

Refresh your browser:

```text
Ctrl + F5
```

or reload the integration.

---

## Knockout Page Appears Empty

Verify:

```text
Settings → Devices & Services
```

shows the World Cup 2026 integration loaded correctly.
