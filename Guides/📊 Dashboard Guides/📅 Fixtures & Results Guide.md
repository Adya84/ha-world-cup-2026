# 📅 Fixtures & Results Guide

The Fixtures & Results page provides a complete view of all FIFA World Cup 2026 matches.

View upcoming fixtures, completed results, kick-off times and match information throughout the tournament.

---

# Accessing Fixtures & Results

Open the World Cup 2026 Application from the Home Assistant sidebar.

Select:

```text
Fixtures & Results
```

from the navigation menu.

---

# What The Fixtures & Results Page Shows

The page combines upcoming fixtures and completed results into a single easy-to-navigate view.

Information may include:

* Match Date
* Kick-Off Time
* Home Team
* Away Team
* Match Status
* Match Result
* Stadium Information
* Tournament Stage

---

# Upcoming Fixtures

Upcoming matches will display:

```text
England vs Brazil
15 June 2026
20:00
```

These fixtures update automatically as official schedules become available.

---

# Match Results

Completed fixtures display final scores.

Example:

```text

```

Results remain available throughout the tournament.

* Match Date

# Troubleshooting

## No Fixtures Showing

Possible causes:

* Tournament data has not yet loaded
* The integration has not updated yet
* The tournament has not started

Allow a few minutes for the first update cycle.

---

## Missing Results

Results only appear once matches have been completed and official data becomes available.

---

## Incorrect Kick-Off Times

Verify your Home Assistant timezone configuration.

Navigate to:

```text
Settings → System → General
```

and confirm your timezone settings are correct.

---

## Page Appears Empty

Refresh your browser:

```text
Ctrl + F5
```

or reload the integration.
